Your empty chairs probably aren't from no-shows.

We audited 25+ dental practices and found the same thing every time: 30-40% of patients are overdue for recall.

They didn't switch dentists. They just forgot. And nobody followed up—because the front desk is too busy putting out fires.

One practice had 900 overdue patients out of 2,400. That's $270K/year in production just sitting there.

Automated recall brought them from 72% to 91% #chairutilization in 90 days. No extra staff. No extra marketing spend.

The biggest unlock? One-tap booking links. Patients will schedule at midnight on their couch—they just won't call your office at 2 PM on a Tuesday.

Worth checking how many of your patients are overdue right now. The number is usually way higher than you think.
