Hot take: most commercial cleaning companies that lose contracts are actually great at cleaning.

They're terrible at communicating.

We audited 20+ cleaning companies and 68% of lost contracts came down to communication—not quality, not price.

Clients don't know if you showed up last night. Complaints sit for 36 hours. Nobody sends a quality report. Ever.

Meanwhile the competitor pitching them is promising "real-time confirmations and monthly reporting."

One company we worked with added automated service confirmations—just a text saying "your building was cleaned at 11:47 PM by [crew lead]."

That one change? Client satisfaction jumped 41%. Contract losses went to zero.

Your clients aren't looking for perfection. They're looking for someone who communicates like they care.

Worth asking your top 5 clients how they'd rate your #communication right now. The answer might surprise you.
