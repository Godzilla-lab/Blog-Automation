hot take: dental appointment reminders actually make no-shows worse.

hear me out.

your practice sends a generic "you have an appointment tomorrow at 2 PM" text. patient sees it. doesn't respond. doesn't show up.

you know what they also ignored today? the pharmacy reminder, the oil change alert, and the weather notification. your text is in the same bucket.

the practices we work with that dropped no-shows from 18% to under 5% all did one thing differently:

they stopped reminding and started confirming.

"reply C to confirm or R to reschedule" — that one change forces a decision. and once someone confirms, they're 70% less likely to bail.

even better: when someone doesn't respond, the system catches it 48 hours out. flags the slot. fills it from the waitlist. the chair was never empty.

one practice recovered $8,400/month just from this shift. the system costs $450.

your patients aren't irresponsible. they just get 80 notifications a day and yours doesn't stand out.

fix the system, not the patient.
