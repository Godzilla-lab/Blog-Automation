I asked a cleaning company owner to track his scheduling time for a week.

He guessed 5-6 hours. Actual number: 18 hours.

Building the schedule. Handling callouts. Texting crews. Fixing mistakes. Reshuffling everything when someone doesn't show up. Repeat forever.

That's a part-time job that produces zero revenue.

The wildest part? His crews were also miserable. They never knew their schedule more than 48 hours out. Routes changed constantly. They were driving 30-40% more than necessary between buildings.

One system change—automated scheduling with route optimization—dropped his time from 18 hours to 2. Crew turnover went from 40% to 12%.

If you run a #cleaningbusiness and scheduling feels like it's eating your life, it probably is. And it's costing way more than you think.
