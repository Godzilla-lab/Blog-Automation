hot take: most automation failures aren't caused by bad tools. they're caused by bad timing.

we've worked with 50+ businesses on AI automation. about a third came to us after a failed first attempt.

the pattern is always the same — they automated a messy process and the mess just got faster.

one dental practice automated reminders on a broken scheduler. a patient got 3 texts with 3 different appointment times. left a 1-star review.

the fix? 2-4 weeks of boring process work BEFORE you automate anything. document it, standardize it, run it manually, then add the tech.

not sexy. but it works 90% of the time vs 40% when you skip it.

automation amplifies what already exists. make sure what exists is actually good first. #AIAutomation #BusinessTips
