I get this question every week: "What does AI automation actually cost?"

Here is the honest answer after 50+ implementations:

Tier 1 (Plug-and-play tools): $200 - $800/month
- Appointment reminders, lead follow-ups, FAQ chatbots
- ROI in 1-2 months
- Best for businesses under 20 employees

Tier 2 (Integrated workflows): $2,000 - $5,000/month
- CRM integration, multi-channel automation, custom AI responses
- ROI in 3-4 months
- Best for 10-50 employee businesses

Tier 3 (Custom AI solutions): $5,000 - $15,000/month
- Custom models, end-to-end automation, predictive analytics
- ROI in 4-6 months
- Best for 50+ employees or high-volume operations

But the monthly price is never the full story.

Hidden costs most vendors will not tell you about:
- Data cleanup (underestimated by 40-60%)
- Staff training (2-4 weeks of reduced productivity)
- Integration with existing tools
- Optimization during months 1-3

The biggest mistake? Comparing AI costs to software subscriptions.

Compare it to labor instead:
- Human agent per interaction: $15-25
- AI per interaction: $1-3

One dental practice client spent $450/month on automated reminders and saved $4,200/month in reduced no-shows. That is a 833% ROI.

The real question is not "what does AI cost?" It is "what does NOT automating cost you every month?"

Start with one high-impact workflow. Budget $500-1,000/month. Measure for 60-90 days. Scale from there.
