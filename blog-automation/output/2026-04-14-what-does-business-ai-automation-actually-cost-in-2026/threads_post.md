Everyone asks "what does AI automation cost?" but nobody gives real numbers.

So here they are:

Basic tools: $200-800/mo (chatbots, reminders, follow-ups)
Integrated workflows: $2,000-5,000/mo
Custom AI: $5,000-15,000/mo

But stop comparing it to software. Compare it to labor.

A human handles one customer interaction for $15-25. AI does it for $1-3.

One of our dental clients spends $450/mo on automated reminders and saves $4,200/mo. That math is not complicated.

The real question is not what AI costs. It is what staying manual costs you every single month.

Start with one workflow. Budget $500-1K. Measure for 90 days. Scale what works.
