1/ "What does AI automation actually cost for a small business?"

After 50+ implementations, here are the real numbers nobody shares. A thread:

2/ Tier 1: $200-800/month

Appointment reminders, lead follow-ups, chatbots.

ROI in 1-2 months. One dental client spent $450/mo and saved $4,200/mo in no-shows.

Start here.

3/ Tier 2: $2,000-5,000/month

CRM integration, multi-channel automation, custom AI workflows.

ROI in 3-4 months. A property manager saved 25 hrs/week in admin - equivalent to $3,000/mo in labor.

4/ Tier 3: $5,000-15,000/month

Custom AI models, end-to-end automation, predictive analytics.

ROI in 4-6 months. The compounding returns make this the cheapest option at scale.

5/ The hidden costs nobody talks about:

- Data cleanup (budget $1K-10K)
- Staff training (2-4 weeks slower)
- System integration ($1K-8K)
- Optimization period (months 1-3)

Skip these and your project stalls.

6/ Stop comparing AI to software. Compare it to labor.

Human per interaction: $15-25
AI per interaction: $1-3

A $1,000/mo AI handling 500 interactions = $2 each.

That is not an expense. It is a multiplier.

7/ The playbook:

1. Pick ONE high-impact workflow
2. Budget $500-1,000/mo
3. Use proven platforms first
4. Measure everything for 60-90 days
5. Scale based on results

The businesses that win are not the ones spending the most. They are the ones starting the smartest.
