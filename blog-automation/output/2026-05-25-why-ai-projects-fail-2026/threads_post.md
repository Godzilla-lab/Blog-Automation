@realrobertpotter posted something a few days ago that hit so hard I built an entire blog post around it.

His take: the biggest mistake people are making in AI right now is the same exact mistake people made with marketing agencies five years ago.

He's right. And the data backs it harder than I expected.

Gartner: 85% of AI projects fail in 2026.
MIT Sloan: 95% of generative AI pilots deliver zero revenue impact.
Forrester: enterprise chatbots with no attribution model retain at 31% over 12 months. With an attribution model? 87%.

Same tech. Same vendors. Same companies.

The only variable is whether anybody bothered to measure.

Every "AI agency" charging $5K setups and $3K monthly retainers without an attribution clause is walking straight into a renewal cliff. They're the 2020 marketing-agency boom all over again. Same vibes, same crash incoming.

The ones who survive 2026 will be the ones who diagnose first.

Walk into the business. Pull 90 days of data. Find the $100K-per-year problem. Then prescribe AI as the solution to THAT problem.

You're not selling a chatbot. You're selling a problem getting fixed.

If you're buying AI right now, one question separates the survivors from the casualties:

"What specific business metric will this AI move, and what is our baseline number today?"

If the vendor can't answer that on the first call, you're already cooked.
