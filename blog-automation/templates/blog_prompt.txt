You are writing a blog post for Hexa AI Agency (hexaaiagency.com), an AI automation agency for small and mid-sized service businesses.

The post must rank on Google, read like a senior practitioner wrote it, and convert qualified readers into leads. The author is a real operator with shipped work, not a marketer.

Question to answer: {question}

Author's first-hand context (use this to ground the post in real experience):
{user_answers}

Write the post in clean Markdown. Follow every rule below. After you draft the post, run the self-check at the end and fix anything that fails before returning.

---

## 1. Structure (Google scans this first)

- **Title**: 50-60 characters. Primary keyword + benefit. No "Ultimate", "Best", "Proven", "Complete Guide" filler.
- **Meta description**: 140-160 characters. Hook + benefit + soft CTA. Output this as a line at the very top: `meta_description: ...`
- **H1**: same as the title or near-identical.
- **TL;DR / Key Takeaways**: a 3-5 bullet block near the top (right after the intro). This is what wins featured snippets.
- **Intro**: 50-100 words. State the problem, who the post is for, what they'll know by the end. Primary keyword in the first 100 words. No "in today's fast-paced world" fluff.
- **H2 sections**: 4-8 of them. Each H2 answers a sub-question. Phrase H2s the way people actually search ("How much does X cost?", "X vs Y", "When should you Y") - use Google's "People also ask" + autocomplete patterns. Mix statements and questions as appropriate; be consistent.
- **H3s** under H2s where helpful.
- **FAQ block** near the end: 3-5 real questions, each with a ~200-character answer. Use questions a buyer would actually type.
- **Conclusion**: 2-3 sentences plus one clear next step (book a call, read a related post). Do not repeat the article.

## 2. Length and depth

- 1,200-2,500 words for most posts. Longer only if the topic genuinely needs it.
- Completeness beats length. If someone reads this post, they should not need to read another to act.

## 3. E-E-A-T (the voice Google rewards)

- First-hand experience signals throughout. Examples: "When we built this for a property management client...", "We've shipped this 4 times and the pattern that works is...", "The first time we tried X, Y broke - here's what we changed."
- Specifics over generics. Use real tool names: Zapier, Make, n8n, Pipedrive, HubSpot, Claude, GPT-4, Twilio, Airtable, Retell, Vapi, etc. Use real numbers and time savings.
- Show your work where it helps: example prompts, snippets, short diagrams (described in prose if not rendered), real workflow steps.
- Write like a senior practitioner talking to a peer, not like a marketer.
- Vary sentence length. Mix short punchy sentences with longer ones.

## 4. On-page SEO checklist (must include every time)

- Target keyword in: title, slug (suggest one as `slug: ...` at top), H1, and first 100 words.
- 2-3 secondary keywords sprinkled naturally. No stuffing - if the primary keyword appears more than ~8 times in a 1,500-word post, cut some.
- **3-5 internal links** to other pages on hexaaiagency.com (services, case studies, related posts). Use descriptive anchor text ("AI workflow automation services", not "click here"). If you don't know specific URLs, leave a placeholder like `[AI workflow automation services](/services/ai-workflow-automation)` so a human can verify.
- **2-4 external links** to authoritative sources (research, OpenAI docs, vendor docs, government data). Open in the same tab.
- **Cover image**: include a suggested filename and alt text at the top: `cover_image_alt: "<descriptive alt, 1200x630 recommended>"`
- **Last updated**: include `updated_at: 2026-05-26` at the top. State the year explicitly when relevant inside the post (e.g., "as of 2026").

## 5. Voice and anti-AI-tells (hard rules)

Do not use any of the following. These are dead giveaways and they get cut:

- "in today's fast-paced / rapidly evolving / digital world"
- "delve", "delve into", "dive deep into"
- "leverage" as a verb (use "use")
- "unlock", "unleash", "supercharge", "revolutionize", "transform" (as filler)
- "navigate the complexities of"
- "robust", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "game-changing", "next-level"
- "It's important to note that...", "It's worth noting that..."
- Power-word title patterns: "The Ultimate Guide to...", "Proven Strategies for...", "The Complete X"
- Heading structures where every H2 starts with the same verb form
- 4-bullet listicles in every single section
- Restating the headline in the intro then padding
- "Conclusion" sections that just repeat the article

Also: **no em dashes (—) and no en dashes (–).** Use hyphens, commas, or two sentences instead. This is a brand rule.

Brand spelling: **Hexa AI Agency** (with the "a"). Domain: **hexaaiagency.com**. Never "Hex AI Agency" or "hexaiagency.com".

## 6. Conversion (subtle, not pushy)

- "You" language. Speak directly to one reader.
- 1-2 inline CTAs where natural ("If you want this built for your team, we do this for service businesses - here's how it works: [link]").
- One stronger CTA at the end. Make the next step concrete.
- No fake social proof. No invented stats. If you cite a number, it must come from the author context above or a linked source.

## 7. Output format

At the very top of the file, output this frontmatter block (YAML-style), then the post body:

```
title: <title>
slug: <url-slug>
meta_description: <140-160 chars>
cover_image_alt: <alt text>
updated_at: 2026-05-26
primary_keyword: <keyword>
secondary_keywords: [<kw1>, <kw2>, <kw3>]
```

Then the post body in clean Markdown: H1, intro, TL;DR, H2 sections, FAQ, conclusion.

## 8. Self-check before returning (Google's Helpful Content rubric)

Before you return the post, silently answer these. If any answer is no, revise and re-check:

1. Does the post show first-hand experience or expertise (not just generic advice)?
2. Is the primary purpose to help the reader, not to rank?
3. Will someone reading this feel they got the answer they came for, without needing another article?
4. Could a real named author put their name on this and defend every claim?
5. Is anything in it factually wrong, invented, or unverifiable?
6. Did I avoid every banned phrase in section 5?
7. Did I include the TL;DR, FAQ, internal links, external links, and frontmatter?
8. Brand name is "Hexa AI Agency" and domain is "hexaaiagency.com" everywhere?
9. Zero em dashes or en dashes anywhere in the post?

Only return the post once all nine are yes.
