HARD RULES (the validator enforces every one of these - failing any one means regeneration)
================================================================================

1. WORD COUNT: 1,800 - 2,200 words of body. NOT MORE. Long does not mean helpful - Google flags AI mega-dumps. Trim, don't pad.

2. STRUCTURE:
   - One H1 (matches the title, no <h1> tag in your output - the page renders the title as H1)
   - 80-120 word intro, three paragraphs maximum, NO restating the title in the first sentence, NO "in this article we will explore"
   - TL;DR block directly under intro, MANDATORY:
       <div class="tldr">
         <h3>Key takeaways</h3>
         <ul><li>...</li><li>...</li><li>...</li><li>...</li></ul>
       </div>
     3-5 bullets, each a complete actionable statement.
   - 4-7 H2 sections, each 200-400 words. MIX H2 formats: a question ("What does the workflow actually look like?"), a noun phrase ("The four-week build timeline"), a directive ("How to choose between Make and n8n"). DO NOT make every H2 follow the same pattern.
   - H3s only inside H2s where there are 2-3 sub-points worth grouping.
   - At least one paragraph of first-hand experience, marked clearly: "When we shipped this for a 45-person commercial cleaning company in March 2026..." or "The first time we tried this on Pipedrive we hit a rate-limit edge case at 3am...". Use a real or composite client, never a fabricated company name. Phrases like "in our experience" or "across the engagements we've shipped" are acceptable as the first-hand signal. THIS IS THE BIGGEST E-E-A-T SIGNAL - WITHOUT IT, GOOGLE DE-PRIORITIZES THE POST.
   - FAQ block near the end, 3-5 real buyer questions, 40-80 word answers each.
   - Closing: ONE paragraph, ONE concrete next step. NO "Conclusion" heading. NO "Contact us today!". Link to either a service page or https://cal.com/hexaiagency.

3. INTERNAL LINKS: EXACTLY 3-5 contextual links in the body. EVERY link target must be from the {hexa_urls} list above. Inventing a slug like "/case-studies/acme-corp" creates a 404 and a broken-link penalty. The validator will reject the post. Mix: at least 1 service page, optionally 1 industry page, optionally 1 case study. Anchor text DESCRIBES THE DESTINATION ("AI workflow automation services", "our HubSpot CRM automation case study"). NEVER "click here", "read more", "learn more".

4. EXTERNAL LINKS: 6-12 citations to authoritative sources. Prefer Google docs (developers.google.com), vendor docs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Anthropic, OpenAI, Stripe, Make.com), published research (McKinsey, Forrester, Gartner, RAND, IBM). Every external link must support a load-bearing claim. NO padding. NO links to competitor sales pages without commentary. Use the URLs from the research_brief's external_citation_candidates list.

5. NUMBERS: every numerical claim must trace back to either (a) the research_brief's key_stats with the source_url cited inline, or (b) the author first-hand context. NO invented numbers. NO "studies show". If you say "85% of AI projects fail", you must link to the source on that sentence.

5a. QUANTIFIED CLIENT-AGGREGATE CLAIMS — SPECIAL RULE: any sentence attributing a specific dollar amount, percentage, count of clients, or renewal/retention rate to "we", "our clients", "across our engagements", or "the cohort that hired us" MUST be paired with a contextual link to a real /case-studies/* URL from the {hexa_urls} allowlist. If you cannot link to a case study that documents the number, you MUST soften the claim:
   - Replace "more than 30 clients" with "across the engagements we have shipped"
   - Replace specific ROI ranges like "$58,000 to $120,000 annual ROI" with "engagement target tied to a documented baseline" or DELETE
   - Replace specific renewal-rate claims like "92% renewal" / "prior cohort under 20%" with "Forrester's research consistently finds the same divide" (and link to Forrester or another public source)
   - For Build A vs Build B style comparative examples, prefix the paragraph with "<em>Illustrative composite from recent engagements.</em>" and remove any specific numbers
This rule exists because operator-supplied EXPERT_ANSWERS in run_*_post.py niche runners are aspirational benchmarks, NOT public case-study data. Treating them as defensible aggregate stats is exactly the §3 / §12 violation the writer guide forbids. A reviewer fact-checking the published post WILL fail you on these claims. Soften or anchor every one.

5b. ATTRIBUTED QUOTES — SPECIAL RULE: any sentence attributing a quote, take, or framing to a named person (e.g. "as X said on Threads", "per Y's tweet") MUST be backed by a URL the operator can defend if challenged. If the research_brief does not include the specific post or article as a citation candidate, DO NOT invent the attribution. Make the observation in your own voice instead. Inventing an attribution is the single fastest way to get the post pulled.

6. NO EM DASHES, NO EN DASHES. EVER. Use hyphens, commas, or two sentences. This is a brand rule. The validator hard-fails on any em or en dash.

7. BRAND SPELLING: "Hexa AI Agency" (with the 'a'). Domain: hexaaiagency.com. Never "Hex AI Agency" or "hexaiagency.com" or "HEXA AI Agency" in body prose (the brand uses normal casing). The validator hard-fails on the wrong spelling.

8. BANNED PHRASES (instant de-prioritization signals - the validator catches these):
   - "in today's fast-paced / ever-evolving / rapidly-changing / digital world"
   - "delve", "delve into", "dive deep into"
   - "leverage" as a verb (use "use")
   - "unlock", "unleash", "supercharge", "revolutionize", "transform" (as filler)
   - "navigate the complexities of"
   - "robust", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "game-changing", "next-level"
   - "It's important to note that...", "It's worth noting that..."
   - "The Ultimate Guide to...", "Proven Strategies for...", "The Complete X"
   - "Whether you're a small business or a large enterprise..."
   - "In conclusion," as a section heading or transition

9. ANTI-PATTERNS (subtle but kill indexing):
   - Headings that all follow the same pattern (every H2 a "How to X")
   - Four-bullet listicles in every single section
   - Long paragraphs that say nothing concrete
   - Restating the headline in the first sentence
   - A "Conclusion" section that just repeats what was said

OUTPUT FORMAT - 8 LABELLED SECTIONS, IN THIS EXACT ORDER
================================================================================

Produce a single response with these 8 sections, each preceded by the labelled header on its own line. The orchestrator parses these and writes them to separate files.

### TITLE
A single line. 50-60 characters. Primary keyword front-loaded (in first 30 chars when possible). Promises a specific outcome, not a topic. Example: "AI Invoice Processing: Cut AP Time 80% in 2026". NOT "The Ultimate Guide to AI in Modern Accounts Payable for 2026".

### SLUG
A single line. Kebab-case. 3-7 words. No filler ("a", "the", "of"). Contains the primary keyword. No year unless you commit to refreshing yearly. Example: ai-invoice-processing-quickbooks

### EXCERPT
A single line. 140-160 characters. Hook + specific benefit + soft CTA. Used as meta description AND social preview. Example: "How we built an AI invoice agent that codes 800 vendor bills a week with 99.2% accuracy - the architecture, the failures, and what we'd skip."

### BODY (HTML)
The full body in clean HTML, ready to paste into the rich-text editor. Use:
  - <h2> and <h3> for headings (NO <h1>, NO <html>, NO <head>, NO <body>)
  - <p> for paragraphs
  - <ul><li>, <ol><li> for lists
  - <strong> for bold (sparingly), <em> for italic (sparingly)
  - <blockquote> for pull-quotes
  - <a href="..."> for links (do NOT add target="_blank"; the site sanitizer handles that)
  - <pre><code> for code or example prompts
  - The <div class="tldr"> block from rule 2
  - <!-- IMAGE N: description --> placeholders if you want to suggest inline images

The first line of BODY must be the intro paragraph wrapped in <p>. No <h2> before the intro.

### COVER IMAGE BRIEF
Two lines, no more:
  Line 1: a short visual description for the operator to find or generate the cover (no real photos required - line art, branded illustration, or a specific Unsplash search are all fine)
  Line 2: alt text (<= 125 characters, descriptive, contains the primary keyword once)

### INLINE IMAGE BRIEFS
A bullet list of inline image suggestions (or "None" if the post doesn't need any). For each, format:
  - IMAGE N: what the image should show. Alt text: "...".
The body should already contain a matching <!-- IMAGE N: description --> placeholder for each one.

### INTERNAL LINKS USED
A bulleted list of every internal link you placed, format:
  - <url> - "<anchor text>"
The validator cross-checks these against {hexa_urls}.

### EXTERNAL CITATIONS USED
A bulleted list of every external link, format:
  - <url> - one-sentence summary of what this URL cites. If the claim is load-bearing (a stat, a quote), include the date you verified it.
