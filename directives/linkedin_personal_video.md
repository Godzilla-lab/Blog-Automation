# Directive: Personal LinkedIn Long-Form Video (Loom-style)

> This is the SOP for the founder's **personal** LinkedIn video content (screen share + face, 3-6 min, 2-3x/week). It is separate from the Hexa AI Agency text-post batches in `workspace/linkedin/*-week-batch/` and from the short Instagram Reels pipeline. Different format, different goal, different audience.

## Goal
Build practitioner authority among **automation builders and peers** by publishing raw, one-take Loom-style videos where the founder shows real work on screen and narrates it. Authority compounds into inbound, partnerships, referrals, and trust-led clients. This is NOT a lead-gen firehose and NOT thought-leadership fluff. It is "here is the actual thing I built, and why."

## When to invoke
User says: "make a LinkedIn video" / "loom video" / "long-form video" / "personal LinkedIn video" / "help me plan this week's videos" / "what video should I make".

## Audience & positioning
- **Audience:** agency owners, ops people, indie builders, anyone building AI automations. Peers, not cold prospects.
- **Positioning:** "The operator who actually builds, showing the real work." No gurus, no theory, no "welcome back to my channel." Pull up a live workflow / prompt / script / dashboard and walk through what it does and *why*.
- **The unfair advantage:** the founder builds real automations every day. Point a screen recorder at the actual work. That single move separates this content from the flood of AI-automation talking heads who only theorize. Every video should lean on "show the real screen."
- **Guardrail:** any "real numbers" content must be about the founder's **own** process/costs, never invented client outcomes or attributed quotes. (See `feedback_blog_no_fabricated_client_stats`.)

## The 4 content pillars
1. **Build teardowns (flagship).** Screen-record an actual automation and narrate it end to end. Highest save-rate and authority. Example: "The n8n flow that turns one blog post into 10 LinkedIn posts."
2. **Tool truth.** Opinions and comparisons, tools shown on screen. Example: "Zapier vs Make vs n8n, what I actually reach for and when."
3. **Building in public.** Agency behind-the-scenes, real systems, what broke this week and the fix, real monthly run costs. Vulnerability plus real numbers builds trust.
4. **Hot takes / lessons.** Contrarian, opinion-led, fast to record (mostly face, one screen example). Travels furthest. Example: "Most 'AI automations' are just Zapier with extra steps, here's the tell."

Topics for each pillar live in `workspace/linkedin/video-idea-bank.md`. Pull from there, mark rows recorded/posted, and keep the bank topped up.

## The one-take video skeleton (never be lost on camera)
1. **Hook (5-10s, face):** say the payoff or the pain immediately. "If your AI agents keep breaking, here's the one fix." No intro fluff, no "hey guys."
2. **Stakes (15-20s, face):** who it's for and why it matters.
3. **The meat (2-4 min, screen):** switch to screen, walk the real thing, narrate what *and why*. Show the parts that broke.
4. **Takeaway (20s, face):** one-sentence summary plus one thing to do.
5. **Soft CTA:** "comment [KEYWORD] for the workflow/prompt/template" (reuses the existing DM funnel) or "follow for more builds."

Keep it tight. 3-6 minutes total. Consistency beats polish.

## Recording setup (minimal, beginner)
- **Tool:** Loom, or Screen Studio (Mac) / OBS (free) for a webcam bubble over the screen.
- **Audio > video:** any decent mic (AirPods/USB). Audio quality matters more than camera quality.
- **Layout:** screen fills the frame, face bubble in a bottom corner. **16:9 landscape** (natural for screen-share; square is an option to test later).
- **Captions:** enable LinkedIn auto-captions on upload (the feed autoplays muted).
- **Batch:** record 2-3 in one sitting to stay ahead of the cadence.

## Posting mechanics (LinkedIn)
- Upload **native video**. Never post a YouTube/external link as the primary content; LinkedIn suppresses reach on external links.
- Write a strong **text hook** above the video: the first 2 lines (before the "see more" cutoff) plus the thumbnail are what drive the play. Reuse the operator-essay voice from the existing batches.
- Length 3-6 min.
- End with a comment-keyword CTA to trigger a DM with the resource (same mechanic as the text-post batches).

## Weekly cadence (repeatable template, no blank page)
- **Video 1 (Mon/Tue):** Build teardown (the meat).
- **Video 2 (Wed/Thu):** Tool truth or Hot take (lighter, faster to record).
- **Video 3 (Fri, optional):** Building-in-public / what-I-learned (reflective, easiest).

Each Monday: pick 2-3 rows from the idea bank, set them for the week, batch-record when possible.

## Edge cases
- **Blank on what to say:** open the idea bank, pick any row in the pillar due that day. Never start from zero.
- **No screen to show (pure hot take):** that's fine for pillar 4, but still cut to one quick screen example if you can; builders trust screens.
- **Video ran long (>6 min):** split into a 2-parter rather than rushing the walkthrough.
- **Nothing built worth showing this week:** fall back to a Tool-truth or Building-in-public topic; those don't require a fresh build.
