# Hexa Funnel Copy , NIGERIAN MARKET ("Leak Report" angle)

**Use with:** `hexa_audit_funnel_backend_spec.md`.
**Market: NIGERIA. Vertical: GENERAL Nigerian companies (mid-to-large), NO niche. Currency: ₦.**
**Offer = BROAD operations leak audit (we fix whatever is leaking). NOT WhatsApp-reply automation , that angle is saturated. Stay flexible on what gets automated.**
**Contact channel = BOTH: a "Chat on WhatsApp" button AND a form/booking (lead picks).**
**HARD RULE: do NOT lead with the word "AI" (scam-coded in Nigeria). Lead with the money/time outcome; say "systems / automation."**
**Proof rule: real Nigerian testimonials (name + city) only. `[REAL_CLIENT_RESULT]` placeholder until verified. No em dashes. Light, tasteful pidgin in hooks only.**

## A. THE PATH (two doors)
```
AD  →  either:
   (1) "Chat on WhatsApp" button  → WhatsApp chat flow (§B)
   (2) "Get My Free Leak Report"  → landing page + short form/booking (§C)
Both → qualification → book a short call OR close.
```

**Chat flow (auto-reply + human):**

5. **If FIT:** "Perfect. Let's do a short 20-minute call to walk through your Leak Report. Pick a time: [link] , or tell me what works."
6. **If NOT fit:** "No wahala , here's a free Business Leak Checklist you can run yourself: [link]. We'll check in later."

## D. QUALIFICATION (chat or form)
- Business type + staff count (fit filter).
- Biggest time-drain right now (intent + pain).
- Timing: now / 1-3 months / just looking.
- **Qualified =** mid-to-large + real pain + now/1-3 months → book/close. **Not qualified** → free Leak Checklist + nurture.
- Only a qualified + booked call fires the conversion event the ads optimise on.

## E. FOLLOW-UP (WhatsApp + SMS + email)
- **Instant:** auto-reply within seconds (the §B.1 greeting for WhatsApp; an instant SMS+email for form leads). Fast reply is a top trust signal in Nigeria.
- **Booked:** confirmation + "what to expect" + ask for [one simple input].
- **Reminders:** day-before + 1-hour-before.
- **No-show:** "sorry we missed you" + easy rebook, 2-3 touches.
- **Unqualified nurture:** the Leak Checklist + a short local case study + soft re-qualify after 1-2 weeks.

## NOTES FOR THE BUILDER
- Two doors: a WhatsApp-button ad path (→ §B chat) and a form/landing path (→ §C). Build both; let the lead pick.
- The OFFER is broad operations automation , the audit fixes whatever leaks. Never pitch it as "WhatsApp automation."
- Bold/bright visuals, real Nigerian faces, local office, mobile-first, 4:5 + 9:16, bias ad delivery to evenings (6-9pm WAT).
- Never lead with "AI." Lead with the money/time leak; "systems/automation" is the mechanism word.
- One LP variant per ad angle (Leak Report, pain-question, no-savings-no-fee, bold-stat, native-founder); they share Sections 2-8 and only swap the Hero.
