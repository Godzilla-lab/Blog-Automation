# Hexa , Operations Leak Report (Deliverable Template)

**What this is:** the productized artifact the free audit produces , the thing shown in the ads/landing page and handed to the client. Built for the **Nigerian market** (₦, broad operations, no "AI" framing, pay-after-savings).
**How to use:** Part 1 is the blank template + how to score and calculate leaks. Part 2 is a filled-in example. Keep it 2-4 pages. Bold/clean, mobile-readable (clients open it on a phone).
**Rule:** every number must be the client's real figure or a clearly-labeled illustrative estimate you walk them through. Never invent results.

## 1. The Score , Operations Efficiency Score: **[XX] / 100  ([Grade])**
> One-line interpretation, e.g. *"[Company] is leaking time and money across [N] areas. The good news: most of it is fixable."*

**How the score works , 5 areas, each scored 0-20:**
| Area | What it measures | Score 0-20 |
|---|---|---|
| **Automation level** | How much repetitive work is automated vs done by hand (0 = all manual, 20 = mostly automated) | __ |
| **Response speed** | How fast enquiries/customers get a reply (0 = hours/days or missed, 20 = within minutes, always) | __ |
| **Systems connectedness** | Are tools connected, or is data re-typed across books/apps? (0 = all separate, 20 = fully integrated) | __ |
| **Follow-up & collections** | Are leads, invoices, and debt (Baki) chased systematically? (0 = ad hoc/forgotten, 20 = nothing slips) | __ |
| **Visibility & reporting** | Does the owner get real-time numbers? (0 = guesswork, 20 = live dashboard) | __ |
| **TOTAL** | | **__ / 100** |

**Grade bands:** 80-100 = A (tight) · 60-79 = B · 40-59 = C · 20-39 = D · 0-19 = F.
*Most businesses land C-D. That gap is the opportunity.*

**How to calculate a leak:**
- **Time leak:** (hours/week spent on the manual task) x (loaded ₦ cost/hour) x 4.33 = ₦/month. Loaded ₦/hour = staff monthly cost / ~173 hours.
- **Direct leak:** uncollected debt not chased, sales lost to slow response, duplicate/error costs , estimate with the client, conservatively.

## Operations Leak Report for **Adigún Distributors Ltd** (Lagos , distribution/trading, ~25 staff)
Prepared by Hexa , [date]
*Where Adigún is losing time and money every month, and what it's worth to fix.*

## 1. Operations Efficiency Score: **36 / 100  (Grade D)**
> Adigún is leaking money across 4 areas, mostly from manual record-keeping and money that isn't chased. Almost all of it is fixable without new staff.

| Area | Score 0-20 |
|---|---|
| Automation level | 6 |
| Response speed | 8 |
| Systems connectedness | 5 |
| Follow-up & collections | 7 |
| Visibility & reporting | 10 |
| **TOTAL** | **36 / 100** |

## 2. Leak Summary
> **Total estimated leak: ~₦920,000 / month** (illustrative).

| # | The leak | Costing you / month | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Manual order & record entry (re-typed across book, Excel, WhatsApp) | ~₦234,000 | High |
| 2 | Uncollected customer debt (Baki) not chased | ~₦450,000 | High |
| 3 | Slow enquiry response , sales lost to faster sellers | ~₦180,000 | Med |
| 4 | Manual daily reports & reminders done by hand | ~₦56,000 | Low |

## 3. The Leaks (detail)
**Leak 1: Manual order & record entry**
- **What's happening:** 3 staff re-type every order into a ledger, then Excel, then confirm on WhatsApp , the same data 3 times.
- **The cost:** ~30 hours/week x ₦900/hour x 4.33 = **~₦234,000/month**.
- **The fix:** one connected order-capture system , enter once, it updates records, stock, and the customer message automatically.
- **Confidence:** High.

**Leak 2: Uncollected debt (Baki)**
- **What's happening:** Credit sales are tracked in a notebook; reminders depend on someone remembering. ~₦450,000 sits uncollected on an average month.
- **The cost:** **~₦450,000/month** tied up (direct cash-flow leak).
- **The fix:** automatic, polite payment reminders on a schedule until paid.
- **Confidence:** High.

**Leak 3: Slow enquiry response**
- **What's happening:** Enquiries pile up; some get answered hours later, some not at all. Estimated ~₦180,000/month in sales walk to faster competitors.
- **The cost:** **~₦180,000/month** (illustrative, agreed with owner).
- **The fix:** instant first-response + routing so no enquiry is missed.
- **Confidence:** Med.

**Leak 4: Manual reports & reminders**
- **What's happening:** The admin and owner spend ~13 hours/week compiling daily sales/stock reports and sending reminders by hand.
- **The cost:** ~13 hours/week x ₦1,000/hour x 4.33 = **~₦56,000/month**.
- **The fix:** automatic daily report + reminders.
- **Confidence:** High.

## 4. Fix Roadmap
| Priority | Fix | Est. monthly saving | Effort | Start? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Automated debt/payment reminders (Baki) | ~₦300,000 recovered | Low | ✅ start here |
| 2 | Connected order & record capture | ~₦234,000 | Med | |
| 3 | Instant enquiry response & routing | ~₦180,000 | Med | |
| 4 | Automatic daily reports | ~₦56,000 | Low | |

**Recommended first fix:** automated debt reminders , lowest effort, fastest cash impact. One win, proven, then we expand.

## 5. Your Next Step
> We'll build the automated debt-reminder system for Adigún. **You pay only after we deliver the savings , no savings, no fee.** We agree today's uncollected-debt baseline, build it, then measure recovered cash after 60 days. You confirm both numbers. Our fee is [set ₦/% here].

## 6. How we measured
- **Baseline:** ~₦450,000 average monthly uncollected debt (from your records, agreed).
- **Savings definition:** extra debt recovered per month vs the baseline, from this process.
- **Window:** 60 days.
- **You keep either way:** this report and a clear map of your operations.
