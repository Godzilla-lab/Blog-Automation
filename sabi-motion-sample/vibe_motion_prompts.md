## Three tips from the workflow (apply every time)
1. **Change details vs. start over:** Change details = swap text/number/color on a good result. New layout or a new element = start fresh (it's faster).
2. **Specific, not round numbers:** "₦1,284,500" reads like a real report; "₦1,250,000" reads made up. Keep them specific.
3. **When it's off, fix the prompt, not Change details:** find the one thing that wasn't specific enough (usually "what happens" or "where it goes"), describe it exactly, re-run.
